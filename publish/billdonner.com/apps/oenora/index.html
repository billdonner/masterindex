Your cellar, understood

Oenora

Turn photographs of your wine collection into a living, trusted cellar that helps you decide what to drink, keep, buy, gift, or explore next.

How it works

Photograph the bottle. Use your iPhone in the cellar. Capture works even with no Internet connection, and the original label photograph is always preserved.

Confirm what Oenora sees. Oenora reads the label and proposes the wine's details. Accept a strong match, compare alternatives, or correct an uncertain result without retyping everything. Nothing becomes part of your cellar until you confirm it.

Understand the collection. Confirmed bottles carry their rack and shelf locations, and unopened wooden cases are tracked right on their shelves. The companion Mac app is the desk: browse the collection as a table, see each rack drawn as a map, and manage locations on the big screen. Everything syncs privately through your own iCloud.

Why Oenora

Photo first. Cataloging should feel like walking the cellar with a camera, not doing data entry at a desk.

Human-confirmed truth. AI can propose an answer, but uncertainty stays visible and corrections remain yours.

Bottle-level knowledge. A wine is not the same as the bottle you own. Oenora keeps location, history, and events connected to the physical bottle - and to the sealed case it may still be resting in.

A durable collection. Original photographs and corrections are retained so the cellar can improve as recognition technology improves.

Private preview
