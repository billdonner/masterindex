# MY PICKLEBALL POWER SERVE
*Right-handed drop serve*

## BEFORE EACH SERVE
1. Left foot forward, right foot back; shoulders 45 degrees sideways.
2. Look at opponent; choose deep target.
3. Drop ball slightly forward of right hip; do not throw.
4. Watch bounce; shift weight right foot to left.
5. Rotate right hip toward net while accelerating paddle.
6. Contact slightly in front of right hip with relaxed arm.
7. Follow through, finish facing net, recover.

## CUE: DROP > SHIFT > ROTATE > HIT > FINISH

## 15-MINUTE PRACTICE
- [ ] 10 slow-motion swings without ball
- [ ] 10 normal serves for baseline
- [ ] 10 serves emphasizing rotation
- [ ] 10 serves with gradually increased speed

**GOAL:** At least 8 of 10 serves legal and deep.

## REMEMBER
- Body rotation, not arm force.
- Rotate during swing, not afterward.
- Keep accuracy; avoid shoulder pain.
